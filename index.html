AI Policy & Framework

Vision

In a future where data aggregation, content generation, automation, and technical execution are abundant, STEM Innovation Academy empowers students to leverage technology as a foundation to master problem framing, ethical judgment, human leadership, and informed decision-making, ensuring they remain thoughtful, responsible, and trusted creators in a rapidly evolving world.

Mission

Our mission is to empower students to become thoughtful, responsible creators who can frame meaningful problems and act with integrity in an AI-rich world. AI is used to support exploration and learning, while human judgment, intellectual agency, and accountability are intentionally preserved.

The Six Pillars

Six areas that guide every AI decision at STEM IA. Staff consider all six together when designing, using, or reviewing AI-supported practice.
